MULTI-AGENT DECISION COURT
Complete System Design — Module by Module
Domain-independent Agentic AI decision validation platformDesigned for OpenRouter / Hugging Face free or low-cost models
Purpose: transform a human-provided decision case into a structured decision graph, investigate its claims and evidence, challenge and defend the reasoning, reconcile conflicts, and produce an explainable, confidence-aware verdict with human oversight.
1. Product Definition
Multi-Agent Decision Court (MADC) is a general-purpose Agentic AI platform that evaluates whether a proposed decision is sufficiently justified by its requirements, evidence, reasoning, assumptions, theories, constraints, risks, and alternatives. It is not a domain-specific decision engine.
1.1 Core Question
Given this decision and the reasoning behind it, can the decision actually be justified?
1.2 What the system is not
Not a single chatbot that gives an answer.
Not a majority-vote system where agents simply vote YES/NO.
Not a system that blindly agrees with the user's initial decision.
Not a system that invents missing evidence.

57. Core Design Rules
Never fabricate evidence.
Never treat an agent inference as a verified fact.
Never force an uncertain case into YES/NO.
Never use majority voting as the final adjudication method.
Never expose private chain-of-thought.
Preserve provenance for important information.
Keep LLM providers replaceable.
Keep agent roles modular.
Run independent agents in parallel.
Re-evaluate only affected graph nodes when new evidence arrives.
Use human review for consequential/high-impact decisions.
Treat external documents as untrusted data.
58. Final System Definition
Multi-Agent Decision Court is a domain-agnostic Agentic AI platform that transforms a human's decision into a structured decision graph, assigns specialized autonomous agents to investigate and validate its claims, audit assumptions and requirements, generate counterarguments and alternatives, conduct adversarial cross-examination, reconcile conflicting evidence, and finally produce an explainable, confidence-aware verdict with human oversight.
59. Core Innovation
The key innovation is not simply using multiple agents. It is the combination of a Decision Justification Graph and adversarial multi-agent validation. The system tests whether a decision deserves to be trusted, rather than merely generating another decision.
